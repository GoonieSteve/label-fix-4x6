**FAQ — Does it work with JADENS?**
Yes, the original utility was developed and tested around a JADENS JD-268BT workflow. Other 4×6 Windows thermal printers may work; always test one label first.

**FAQ — Can it automatically print every label I download?**
It can watch a selected folder for new PDF files and, when you opt in and configure SumatraPDF and your printer, automatically submit recognized shipping labels for printing. Unknown files are skipped.

**FAQ — Does it read my Gmail or Vinted account?**
No. Download your labels as PDFs yourself. The app doesn't connect to email or shopping accounts or upload your documents.

**FAQ — Does it cost anything?**
No. LabelFix is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.
